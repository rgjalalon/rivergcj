No. 01
Eat for the long game.
Gisele Bündchen, 46
A plant-forward, Mediterranean-style diet for over a decade. Vegetables, good fats, almost no processed food. She’s called food her medicine.
~30%
fewer major cardiovascular events on a Mediterranean diet with olive oil or nuts, in the PREDIMED trial of about 7,400 people.
New England Journal of Medicine, 2018

No. 02
Choose consistency over intensity.
David Beckham, 51
Strength sessions, ice baths, daily discipline. He’s said staying in shape stopped being about football a long time ago. Now it’s about being healthy for his family.
10–17%
lower risk of early death with regular muscle-strengthening activity.
British Journal of Sports Medicine, 2022

03 / 04
No. 03
Recover smarter than you train.
Stephen Curry, 38
His edge isn’t talent anymore, it’s recovery. Float tanks, mobility work, strict sleep, and a training staff that treats his body like a long-term investment.
199
cohort studies (20.9 million observations) found cardiorespiratory fitness is one of the strongest predictors of living longer.
British Journal of Sports Medicine, 2024

04 / 04
No. 04
Build strength you can retire on.
Serena Williams, 44
She won a Grand Slam at 35, while pregnant, on a foundation of strength training. Muscle isn’t aesthetics. It’s insurance.
In older adults, more muscle mass was linked to significantly lower mortality, independent of weight.
American Journal of Medicine, 2014
