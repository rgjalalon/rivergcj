What a drink can't do
The drink can be a healthy part of your routine. But it can't tell you what your cortisol levels are.
No study has shown it actually lowers cortisol.

The only way to know your levels is to measure them. Testing at a few points shows your full daily curve, not just one moment.
And the shape matters: research links a flatter curve, where levels barely drop from morning to night, to poorer health.
