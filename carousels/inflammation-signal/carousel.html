Week-to-week swing of hs-CRP in healthy people

The standard inflammation test is hs-CRP. It spikes with illness, stress, or just a bad week.

So one high reading may not show your baseline.

02  ·  The alternative03 / 06
Meet the marker
GlycA
Short for glycoprotein acetylation

One signal that reads several inflammation proteins at once, from the same blood draw.

Jim Otvos, who developed the method, says it gives a fuller picture of inflammation than any single marker.

Jim Otvos, Ph.D.Otvos JD et al.

Week to week, same healthy people

A steadier marker is easier to follow over time.

hs-CRP

Roughly 7× the swing

Week to week, GlycA moved 4.3% and hs‑CRP moved 29.2% in the same healthy people. That steadiness is what tracking chronic inflammation needs.

SourceConnelly MA et al. Clinica Chimica Acta, 2016
