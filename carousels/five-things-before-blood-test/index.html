Before your blood test01 / 05
01
Only fast if
you're asked to.
Some tests need you to stop eating for up to 12 hours. Many don't. When you do fast, keep drinking water.
Source: NHS, Blood tests
Before your blood test
02
Skip the
hard workout.
In one study, a weightlifting session raised liver enzymes in healthy men for at least a week.
Keep it gentle for a couple of days before your test.
Source: Pettersson J et al. 2008, British Journal of Clinical Pharmacology

Before your blood test03 / 05
03
Book a
morning slot.
Some hormones change through the day. Guidelines recommend testing testosterone in the morning, when levels are highest.
Source: Bhasin S et al. 2018, Journal of Clinical Endocrinology & Metabolism
Before your blood test04 / 05
04
Pause your
hair gummies.
High-dose biotin can interfere with some blood tests, including thyroid tests.
Pause it for three days, and tell your doctor everything you take.
Source: FDA, 2017, updated 2019
Before your blood test
05 / 05
05
Just been ill?
Say so.
A cold or infection can push up inflammation markers like hsCRP for a while. Your doctor may suggest testing once you've recovered.
Source: Pearson TA et al. 2003, Circulation
The takeaway
Prepared,
not perfect.
A little preparation means your results show your real baseline, not just your week.
